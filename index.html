隐私政策

梦迹 DreamTrace · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日

本页与应用内隐私政策一致。梦迹没有账号、广告、跟踪 SDK 或开发者运营的数据服务器。

本机信息

梦境文字、原声、解读、旧版本、后记、收藏、梦类型、个人符号含义和睡眠记录保存在应用沙盒。生日只保存月和日，不含年份。偏好、语言与权限选择保存在本机。开发者不会收到这些数据。

麦克风与 Apple 语音

只有你开始录音时才使用麦克风。默认要求本机识别；若本机不支持，保留录音并提示重试。只有你主动打开允许 Apple 在线转写且本机无法识别时，语音才可能发给 Apple，依 Apple 语音服务政策处理。开发者不接收语音。可随时关闭该选项或直接打字。

通知和应用锁

提醒由本机定时，需通知授权。应用锁使用系统密码或生物识别；应用不读取生物特征模板。后台会遮住内容。应用锁不是导出文件的独立加密。

备份、导入与分享

完整备份包含私密原声和文字，为明文文件；生日可选择包含。导入前显示预检，冲突保留副本。只有你选择分享目的地后才交出文件。iOS 设备或 iCloud 系统备份可能包含应用数据，取决于你的系统设置；这不同于应用自行云同步。

删除范围

单条删除进入最近删除，可恢复。永久删除会清理该条数据及不再被引用的原声。清空个人数据还会移除修订、后记、生日、作息、符号含义、恢复缓存与应用内临时导出文件。外部保存的导出副本和系统备份需你在相应服务中管理。开发者没有可恢复的副本。

支持邮件与网站

主动发送支持邮件时，邮件地址与邮件内容会送达 lixiangyu1900@gmail.com；请仅提供你愿意发送的资料。打开公开政策或 Apple 链接时，对方网站可能收到常规网络请求信息。

你的选择与儿童

可查看、备份和删除本机数据，不填写生日，不启用提醒，不授权录音。应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。适用法律赋予的权利不受影响。因为开发者不持有日记，请在本机完成日记的查阅和删除。

联系与变更

隐私问题：lixiangyu1900@gmail.com。数据处理有实质变化时会更新版本日期和说明。

Privacy Policy

DreamTrace · Effective date: October 25, 2026

This page matches the privacy policy in the app. DreamTrace has no account, ads, tracking SDK, or developer-operated data server.

On-device information

Dream text, audio, readings, old versions, reflections, favorites, dream types, personal symbol meanings, and sleep notes are stored in the app sandbox. Birthday stores month and day only, without a year. Preferences and language choices are local. The developer does not receive these data.

Microphone and Apple speech

The microphone is used only when you start recording. Recognition is on-device by default; unsupported recognition leaves audio saved for later. Only if you explicitly allow Apple online transcription and on-device recognition is unavailable may audio be sent to Apple, under Apple’s speech-service policies. The developer does not receive audio. Turn this option off at any time or type instead.

Notifications and app lock

Reminders are scheduled locally with your notification permission. App lock uses the device passcode or biometrics; the app does not read biometric templates. Content is hidden in the background. App lock is not separate encryption for exported files.

Backup, import, and sharing

Full backups contain private audio and text in an unencrypted file, optionally including birthday. Imports show a preview and keep conflicts as copies. Files are shared with the destination you select. iOS device or iCloud system backups may include app data depending on your system settings; this is distinct from app-operated cloud sync.

Deletion scope

Deleting an entry moves it to Recently Deleted for recovery. Permanent deletion removes the entry and audio no longer referenced by other entries. Clearing personal data also removes revisions, reflections, birthday, sleep details, symbol meanings, recovery files, and temporary exports in the app. Manage external export copies and device backups in their respective services. The developer holds no recovery copy.

Support email and websites

If you send support email, your email address and message reach lixiangyu1900@gmail.com; include only what you intend to send. Public policy and Apple links may expose ordinary web-request information to those websites.

Your choices and children

View, back up, and delete local data, skip birthday or reminders, and decline recording permissions. The app is not directed to children under 13. Rights under applicable law remain unaffected. Because the developer has no journal copy, access and deletion of journal data happen on your device.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions: lixiangyu1900@gmail.com. Material changes to data handling will update the policy date and description.
